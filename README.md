# JikKey 3D Models

Printable STL models and STEP geometry for JikKey, ShieldSigner, and DiceBox.

- [ShieldSigner Secret Case](./ShieldSigner%20Secret%20Case/): screwless case with removable upper and lower trays, including hidden SIM and microSD storage.
- [DiceBox Hidden Lock](./DiceBox%20Hidden%20Lock/): a 58 × 58 × 15.6 mm PLA dice shaker for twenty-five 5 mm dice and a 50 × 50 × 2 mm clear panel, with concealed lid latches.
